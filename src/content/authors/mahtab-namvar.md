Mahtab Namvar is the founder and voice behind Becoming Elysian: a wellness space for women who want to feel stronger, steadier, and more at home in their bodies without living under the pressure of perfection.

Based in the Canary Islands and known online as [@mahtab_nmvr](https://www.instagram.com/mahtab_nmvr/), Mahtab brings together fitness, nutrition, mobility, and grounded self-care through a practical, feminine, and evidence-aware lens. Her work is shaped by a simple belief: health should support a full life, not shrink it.

As an ISSA fitness and nutrition coach, Mahtab writes about the habits that help women build durable energy and confidence: protein-rich meals, strength training, blood sugar balance, mobility, recovery, stress resilience, and a kinder relationship with food and body change.

Her approach is warm but clear. No crash plans. No all-or-nothing rules. Just thoughtful guidance for women who want to nourish well, move with intention, and build a version of wellness that feels sustainable in real life.
