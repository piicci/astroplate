Protein supports muscle, recovery, appetite, immune function, and metabolic health. For women over 35, it becomes especially useful because muscle is one of the strongest anchors for long-term energy and resilience.

The goal is not to obsess over numbers. A steadier approach is to include a meaningful protein source at most meals, then build the rest of the plate with plants, fiber-rich carbohydrates, and fats that help the meal feel satisfying.

This guide will help you think about protein with clarity, flexibility, and less noise.
