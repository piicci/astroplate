Nutrition after 35 does not need to become more restrictive. It often needs to become more supportive.

The foundation is simple: enough protein, steady meals, colorful plants, healthy fats, fiber-rich carbohydrates, hydration, and a rhythm that helps blood sugar and energy feel more stable across the day.

This article is a starting point for eating in a way that supports strength, hormones, digestion, and long-term wellbeing without turning food into another source of pressure.
