Strength training is not only about changing how your body looks. It is also about supporting bone density, metabolic health, posture, confidence, and the feeling that your body can carry you through real life.

For women over 35, the best routine is usually not the most intense one. It is the one that is progressive, recoverable, and repeatable.

Start with the basics, give your body time to adapt, and let strength become a supportive practice rather than another demand.
